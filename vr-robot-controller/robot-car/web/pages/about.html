About the project

Many small robots are controlled the same way: a few motors and a camera, run by a small computer. This project builds one VR controller for robots like these, starting with a Raspberry Pi robot car.

What it does

See through the robot's camera

The camera appears on a screen floating in your real room (mixed reality). With head-look on, the camera turns when you turn your head.

Drive with the Quest controllers

Left stick drives forward and back, right stick turns. If the connection drops for 0.3 seconds, the robot stops by itself.

Safety sensors

Ultrasonic sensors slow the robot as it backs toward something, stop it before it hits, and stop it at a table edge. The headset shows a parking-sensor display and beeps.

How it works

The headset's "app" is a web page the Pi serves, so there's nothing to install on the Quest. Video and controls are separate programs, so a video slowdown can never delay a steering command or the safety stop.
